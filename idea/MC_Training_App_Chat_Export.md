### 🔥 Some of the most iconic ones

**1. Saitama — One Punch Man**

The classic:

> 100 push-ups  
> 100 sit-ups  
> 100 squats  
> 10 km running  
> **Every single day**

Plus his famous rule of **no air conditioning** and eating a banana for breakfast.

---

**2. Netero — Hunter × Hunter**

Probably one of the craziest.

Netero trained by performing **10,000 punches followed by a prayer every day**.

The important part isn't just the number of punches — the training was designed to make the movement and prayer essentially automatic.

---

**3. Rock Lee — Naruto**

Rock Lee's whole character is basically **"train harder than everyone else."**

His training involves huge amounts of:

- Running
- Push-ups
- Squats
- Kicking
- Handstand exercises
- Weight training
- Sparring

And he famously trains with **ankle weights**, making his normal movement dramatically harder.

---

**4. Izuku Midoriya — My Hero Academia**

Deku's first major training program is actually pretty recognizable as a real fitness program.

He spends **10 months cleaning Dagobah Beach**, while progressively moving huge amounts of debris.

It develops his:

- Legs
- Back
- Core
- Grip
- Cardiovascular conditioning
- Overall strength

Basically a giant functional-fitness program.

---

**5. Goku / Vegeta — Dragon Ball**

Their training is less like a normal gym routine and more like **"destroy your body under impossible conditions."**

Examples include:

- Gravity training
- Weighted clothing
- Fighting at high speed
- Martial arts
- Endurance training
- Sparring
- Extreme recovery cycles

Vegeta especially is famous for using **high-gravity environments**.

## Assistant

Yes. This could work really well if you treat it as **gamification + progressive fitness**, rather than simply copying anime workouts.

The core idea I'd use is:

> **You start as a Side Character. Your goal is to become the Main Character.**

Each anime character becomes a **training path**, while the overall app has a universal **Level + Rank system**.

### Gravity Chamber = Progressive Resistance

Instead of 10× gravity:

# One important design decision

I **wouldn't actually make the anime workouts the primary training programs**.

I'd make them the **theme layer**.

Underneath, the app should have a legitimate progressive fitness engine.

For example:

> User chooses **Vegeta**

The user thinks they're doing:

**Gravity Chamber Training**

But underneath, the program is intelligently progressing:

**Squat 40 kg → 45 kg → 50 kg → 55 kg**

That's far better than telling someone:

> "Do 500 squats because Vegeta would."
